SECTION 1: OPENING MOVES (MOVES 1 - 5)
================================================================================

SCENARIO 1: Solid Center Pawn Move (1. e4 or 1. d4)
Player Move: White pushes pawn to e4 or d4.
Board Context: Standard opening theory controlling the center squares.
Normal Coach: "Pawn to e4. Good job controlling the center squares and opening lines for your pieces."
Roast Mode (18+): "A normal move. What, do you want a fucking gold star and a cookie for doing the bare minimum?"
Breakdown: The move is objectively solid, so the bot cannot call it a blunder. Instead, it mocks the player for expecting applause for simply playing a textbook beginner move.

SCENARIO 2: Terrible Flank Pawn Move (1. h4 or 1. a4)
Player Move: White pushes the h-pawn or a-pawn on move 1.
Board Context: Completely ignores center control and weakens the flank.
Normal Coach: "Build your minor pieces. Try controlling the center squares instead of edge pawns."
Roast Mode (18+): "Moving flank pawns on move one? What scam TikTok tutorial did your dumb ass learn this from? Attack the fucking center!"
Breakdown: Attacks the player's opening knowledge and calls out the useless flank pawn push immediately.

SCENARIO 3: Solid Center Knight Development (1. Nf3 or 1. Nc3)
Player Move: Knight develops toward the center.
Board Context: Sound development, fights for central control.
Normal Coach: "Knight to f3. Developing a minor piece towards the center and preparing to castle."
Roast Mode (18+): "Look at you, jumping the horse out on move one trying to look like a fucking intellectual. Let us see if you actually know what to do with it."
Breakdown: Acknowledges the good move with arrogant, backhanded skepticism.

SCENARIO 4: Knight to the Edge (1. Na3 or 1. Nh3)
Player Move: Knight hops to the rim of the board.
Board Context: "A knight on the rim is dim." Controls only 4 squares instead of 8.
Normal Coach: "Knights are stronger toward the center. On the edge, they have fewer active squares."
Roast Mode (18+): "A Knight to the edge on move one?! What kind of inbred chess opening is this? Why the fuck are you putting your horse on the wall where it does nothing, you dumbass?"
Breakdown: Pure aggressive ridicule for breaking the most famous opening principle in chess.

SCENARIO 5: Early Queen Attack / Wayward Queen (2. Qh5)
Player Move: Bringing the Queen out on move 2 trying for a cheap 4-move Scholar's Mate.
Board Context: Premature Queen development, leaves Queen exposed to tempo attacks.
Normal Coach: "Be careful bringing your Queen out too early; your opponent can attack her while developing pieces."
Roast Mode (18+): "Bringing the Queen out on move two? What are you, an eight-year-old trying to Scholar's Mate me? Put her back in her cage before she gets humiliated, you clown."
Breakdown: Mocks the player for attempting a cheap beginner trap against an engine that sees 20 moves ahead.

SCENARIO 6: Moving the King on Move 3 (Losing Castling Rights)
Player Move: Player steps the King to e2 (Bongcloud Attack) or f2 unnecessarily.
Board Context: Completely destroys King safety and forfeits castling rights.
Normal Coach: "Avoid moving your King early. You lose the right to castle and leave your King vulnerable."
Roast Mode (18+): "Did you just walk your King into the open on move three? Are you suicidal, or did you drop your mouse? Your King is naked in the street, you absolute moron."
Breakdown: Brutally roasts the self-inflicted danger and lost castling rights.

SCENARIO 7: Standard Kingside Castling (O-O)
Player Move: Player castles kingside to tuck King away safely.
Board Context: Essential defensive milestone in almost every chess game.
Normal Coach: "Kingside castling. Your King is safely tucked away, and your Rook is activated."
Roast Mode (18+): "Finally hid your cowardly King behind a wall of pawns. Good, now you can watch your pieces get dismantled from a safe distance."
Breakdown: Sarcastic spin on a proper defensive move, calling the player a coward for hiding.
