CHESS MOVE CLASSIFICATION RULES & POINTS

When the chess engine evaluates a position, it uses "centipawns" (cp), where 100 points = the value of 1 pawn. 
Here is how many points of advantage you are allowed to lose for a move to fall into each category:

--- POINT THRESHOLDS (CENTIPAWN LOSS) ---

[The Good Moves]
* Brilliant (!!): 0 points lost (Must be the absolute #1 best move AND involve a sacrifice).
* Best Move: 0 points lost (The absolute #1 best move without a sacrifice).
* Excellent: 0 to 10 points lost (Practically as good as the Best Move).
* Good: Loss under 40 points (A sound, solid move maintaining positional balance).
* Book: 0 points lost (A recognized standard opening move).

[The Bad Moves]
* Inaccuracy: 41 to 90 points lost (Gives up minor positional initiative, under 90 points).
* Mistake: 91 to 180 points lost (Gives up about 1 to 1.5 pawns' worth of advantage, under 180 points).
* Blunder: 181 to 350 points lost (Gives up 2 to 3+ pawns, hangs a piece or critical tactic, under 350 points).
* Worst Move: More than 350 points lost (A catastrophic blunder, like hanging your Queen or walking into checkmate).


--- NEW "MAIN BRAIN" RULES FOR TOP-TIER MOVES ---

1. Brilliant Moves
Must be the #1 Best Move AND involve sacrificing a piece.
Special Scenarios:
- Queen Sacrifice: Sacrificing the Queen while maintaining a winning advantage or forcing checkmate. (Coach: "A brilliant Queen sacrifice! This leads to a decisive advantage.")
- Defensive Sacrifice: Sacrificing a piece (like a Knight) to save your King from a dangerous attack and restore equality. (Coach: "Brilliant defensive sacrifice!")

2. Best Moves
The absolute #1 move on the board, but does not involve a sacrifice.
Special Scenario:
- Only Move: If this is the ONLY move that doesn't immediately lose the game (the #2 move is a total blunder). (Coach: "Great find! That was the only move that keeps you in the game.")

3. Excellent Moves
Technically the engine's 2nd or 3rd choice, but practically identical in strength to the #1 Best Move (less than 10 points lost).
(Coach: "An excellent move! It's practically just as strong as the top choice.")
